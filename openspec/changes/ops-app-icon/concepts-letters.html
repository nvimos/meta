Ops letter icons

The word ops drawn in one piece. Every stroke is a flat slab whose end is cut on the 45° line, the way Flutter's bars end. Colours follow Neovim: green on the upper left, blue on the lower right, with a lighter sky-blue ribbon where the two meet. The o and p are octagonal rings, the s is two bars joined by a 45° diagonal.

ops all three letters on a tile

The full word. At 32 px and up it reads clearly. At 16 px the strokes are about one pixel wide, so the letters turn into coloured blocks.

op first two letters, bigger

Only the o and p, so each stroke is about half as wide again. The p's pointed stem and the o's folded corner stay visible down to 16 px. It reads as the start of "ops" rather than the full name.

o one letter, full tile

A single octagonal ring, green on one side and blue on the other, with the sky-blue fold in the corner. The sturdiest at 16 px and the most like a logo, but the least tied to the name.
