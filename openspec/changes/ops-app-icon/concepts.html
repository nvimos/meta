Ops icon concepts

Both logos are built from flat slanted slabs and a 45° fold. Flutter has blue bars folding into a chevron; Neovim has green and blue pillars joined by a diagonal. Each concept below borrows that geometry without copying either mark, and each is shown at the sizes Windows actually uses (taskbar 32, title bar 16).

A. Prompt chevron + cursor

A Flutter-style folded chevron as a shell prompt, followed by Neovim's green block cursor, on a dark tile. It reads as "an app that drives an editor" and stays clear at 16 px because the tile gives it a solid outline on any taskbar.

B. Slab N two pillars, ribbon diagonal

The closest to both parents: green and blue pillars, with a light Flutter ribbon as the diagonal, and a shade where it folds over the pillar. It is the most recognizable, which also means it sits closest to the Neovim mark.

C. Diamond rotated tile, chevron cut

A diamond split green and blue down the middle, with a white chevron punched through. Bold at small sizes; the least literal of the four.

D. Seam split tile, cursor in the seam

A rounded tile cut on the 45° line into the backend's green and the front end's blue, with a light ribbon along the cut and a block cursor on the blue side. It matches the modern Windows tile style best, but loses the most detail at 16 px.
